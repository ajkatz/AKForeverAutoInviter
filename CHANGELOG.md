# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- First build: invite by keyword from whispers, guild, say/yell or public channels.
- Class list, level range, only guild members and/or friends, full party -> raid.
- A stranger's level by /who on your next key press; guild members' and friends' levels from the rosters.
- Party -> raid: when full, or any party you lead (`/autoinv raid always`).
- Raid assistant for everyone, or for a list of names (`/autoinv promote`).
- Settings window (`/autoinv`), recent decisions, `/autoinv log`, `/autoinv diag`.
